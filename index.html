Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the frequently asked questions

What is roadmap.sh?

roadmap.sh is a community effort to create learning paths, guides, project ideas and other similar content to help developers grow in their careers.

What are the plans for roadmap.sh?

We plan to expand our role-based roadmaps, skill-based guides, interactive learning tools, and project-based challenges.

How is roadmap.sh built?

roadmap.sh is built as an open-source project primarily using Astro, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.

Can I use roadmap.sh in my team?

Yes, many teams and organizations use the roadmaps to guide career progression, onboarding, and internal skill mapping.

How can I create custom roadmaps?

You can use the roadmap editor directly on the site to design custom learning tracks, publish them, or keep them private.

Is roadmap.sh really 7th most starred project on GitHub?

Yes! The repository is consistently among the top 10 most starred repositories across all of GitHub.
